## Attribution and notices

Keep upstream copyright, license, and attribution text intact in files carrying it. When redistributing a source file under its embedded grant, include the required notices in copies and substantial portions. Retain the `enum34` and EPL notices for those distinct components and meet their respective license conditions. A project-level notice must not replace, remove, or absorb these third-party notices.

## Open redistribution questions

- The checked-out Advantech example bundle has no top-level license/readme defining terms for every file. Advantech's public product/support pages establish origin and package purpose, but the pages reviewed do not grant redistribution rights for the complete bundle or its generated/binary contents.
- The source-level Advantech grant appears only in files that contain it; older copyright-only example files are not covered by that evidence.
- No written permission for placing the complete SDK example bundle or the manual in this repository was found during this inventory. Confirm with the repository owner whether such permission or separate acquisition terms exist before public redistribution.

## Project copyright notice boundary

Any project-owner copyright notice should identify only original project-authored material and its authorship scope. Do not apply it to `references/advantech_sdk/`, the manual PDF, or third-party components, and do not imply ownership of Advantech or other upstream work. Preserve the local notices in place.
